Kasino — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Kasino (“the app”) is a card game. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: Kasino does not require an account and does not collect personal information for advertising or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • A display name you choose (used to label you in a game).
  • A random, anonymous player ID (so the game can recognise you when you rejoin a room).
  • Your settings and the state of a game in progress.

This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Online multiplayer

When you host or join an online game, the app connects to a relay server to pass game data between the players in your room. During a session it transmits your chosen display name, your anonymous player ID, the room code, and the moves and state of the card game. This data is used solely to run the multiplayer session between the players in that room. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Session data on the server is transient and is not retained beyond the game session.

You can play entirely offline against the computer, in which case no data leaves your device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or collection of your name, email, or contacts.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your camera, Bluetooth, microphone, location, or photos.

Children

Kasino collects no personal information and is suitable for players of all ages.

Your choices

Delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Since online play requires no account, there is no separate profile to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Kasino — Support

The classic card game.

Need a hand with Kasino? Here are the basics — and how to reach us.

Getting started

  • How to play: tap INSTRUCTIONS on the menu for the full rules, or HOW TO PLAY to watch a guided round.
  • Play on your own: tap NEW GAME, set Players to 1, and play against the computer.
  • Play with friends: one player taps NEW GAME to get a room code; the others tap JOIN GAME and enter it. 2–4 players, including 2 vs 2 teams.
  • Pick up where you left off: your game resumes automatically if you switch away and come back.

Scoring

Points go to most cards, most spades, each sweep, each ace, the last trick, Little Kasino (the 2 of spades) and Big Kasino (the 10 of diamonds, worth 2). Most points after the rounds wins.
